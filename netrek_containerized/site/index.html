02Get Access — Whitelist Required

This server only accepts connections from approved IP addresses. Before you can connect, your public IP has to be added to the allow-list.

  1. Find your public IP: open checkip.amazonaws.com (or search “what is my IP”).
  2. Email it to dave@elevateconsulting.dev and ask to be whitelisted.
  3. Once you get the go-ahead, connect with any client below.

Tip: home IPs can change. If you suddenly can't connect, re-check your IP and email again.

04Connect

  1. Make sure your IP is whitelisted (step 02) and the server is up for your session.
  2. Point your client at server sturgeon.elevateconsulting.dev, port 2592.
  3. Pick a team, press a fighter slot to enter, and fly.

Recommended client setting for this server: tryShort: off in your .netrekrc (avoids a short-packet stream desync). UDP is fine to leave on.
